That's the risk of studying without a clear plan. Find your weak points first, then spend your time fixing them—not guessing.

You don't have to guess. We look at your actual performance, find the gaps, and show you what to work on next.

You don't need perfect English. You need the right skills for the score you want. We focus on the gaps between your current level and your target.
