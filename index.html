Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your diagnostic.

What happens during the diagnostic? +

We discuss your goal, work through targeted IELTS tasks, and identify where your preparation needs the most attention.

Will I get an exact IELTS band score? +

No. This is a focused diagnostic, not a full IELTS examination. The goal is to identify the areas most likely limiting your performance and give you a clear direction for preparation.

Do I need to prepare before the session? +

No. Come as you are. Your natural performance gives us more useful information.

What happens after the diagnostic? +

You'll know what to focus on next. If you need further support, we'll determine the best path from there.

How long will it take me to get the score I need? +

It depends on where you're starting and where you need to go. Your diagnostic gives me a clearer picture of the gap—and the work needed to close it.
